NARUTO VERSE

Naruto is a Japanese Manga series authored and illustrated by Masashi Kishimoto, a mangaka working for Weekly Shōnen Jump magazine.
The main story follows Naruto Uzumaki, a young outcast ninja from the Konohagakure (Village Hidden in the Leaves), as he aspires to become the Hokage, or the renowned leader of the village, to spite the village that rejected him and become a person acknowledged by all. Naruto, alongside his rival Sasuke Uchiha and Sakura Haruno, are assigned to form a three-person squad named Team 7 under the direct supervision of experienced Shinobi, Kakashi Hatake to face many challenges as they learn what it means to be a Shinobi. This brings Naruto and his group in direct contact with the dark nature of Shinobi World, from survivors of war-torn countries to those who wish to destroy the world for the sake of revenge.
After the manga ended, the series received additional canon materials, including The Last: Naruto the Movie, several light novels named the Naruto Hiden Series, the prequel One-Shot manga Naruto: The Whorl within the Spiral and even a short sequel manga called Naruto Gaiden: The Seventh Hokage and the Scarlet Spring.
Eventually a full-fledged sequel series for the francise was created through Boruto: Naruto Next Generations, which tells the story of Boruto Uzumaki, son of the now Seventh Hokage Naruto Uzumaki, as he goes on his own journey of becoming a Shinobi, encountering various foes including rouge Shinobi, cybernetically enhanced life forms, and members of the extraterrestrial Otsutsuki Clan trying to rekindle their attempts to destroy the world for the sake of evolution.
